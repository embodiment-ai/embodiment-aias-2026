# AIAS+ 2026 Workshop: Internal Agenda & Run-of-Show
## Embodiment in Multimodal Large Language Models

* **Date & Time:** Saturday, November 7, 2026 | 10:10 AM – 12:10 PM PST (120 Minutes)
* **Venue:** The Ritz-Carlton, San Francisco (AIAS+ 2026 Conference)
* **Organizers:** Akila Kadambi (Google DeepMind / UCLA), Marco Iacoboni (UCLA), Lisa Aziz-Zadeh (USC), Srini Narayanan (Google DeepMind)
* **Website:** https://embodiment-ai.github.io/embodiment-aias-2026/
* **Goal & Output:** Convening leaders across AI, neuroscience, and cognitive robotics to translate the Dual-Embodiment framework (Kadambi et al., *Neuron*, 2026) into concrete computational architectures, homeostatic AI safety, and a co-authored *Neuron* Perspective / Community Roadmap Paper.

## 🎯 Key Panel Discussion Prompts (for 11:20 – 11:55 AM)

1. **The Representation Gap:** How do we transition from passive token prediction and static visual embeddings to continuous, self-regulating internal states in foundation models?
2. **Intrinsic Alignment:** Can biological homeostasis provide a more robust mathematical foundation for AI safety and prosocial behavior than external reward tuning (RLHF/DPO)?
3. **Benchmark Standards:** What simulation environments or empirical benchmarks can definitively measure whether an artificial model possesses functional internal embodiment vs. superficial behavioral imitation?
4. **Roadmap Objectives:** What are the three most urgent open research problems our post-workshop *Neuron* Perspective paper must articulate?
