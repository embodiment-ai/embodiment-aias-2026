### Executive Summary & Motivation
Current Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved unprecedented performance in symbolic processing and cross-modal reasoning (text, vision, audio). However, despite these successes, current foundation models lack **bodily experience**—understanding the world primarily through disembodied, passive statistical associations.

In our recent *Neuron* paper (*Kadambi et al., 2026*), we introduced the **Dual-Embodiment Framework**, positing that true world comprehension and robust agency require bridging **external embodiment** (sensory-motor interactions with the external environment) and **internal embodiment** (internal state dynamics, proprioception, and homeostatic regulation). 

Building upon the upcoming **AIAS+ 2026 Workshop on Embodiment in MLLMs** (November 7, 2026, San Francisco), this proposed **Neuron Perspective** synthesizes consensus insights from leading AI, neuroscience, and robotics researchers to provide a concrete, operationalized roadmap for incorporating internal state dynamics into next-generation AI architectures.

---

### Core Themes & Perspective Structure

#### 1. Beyond Disembodied Statistical Associations: The Case for Internal Embodiment
- Limitations of purely external token modeling in current multimodal AI.
- Neurobiological foundations: Interoception, homeostatic drives, and continuous physiological regulation as core drivers of biological intelligence and felt cognition.

#### 2. Computational Architectures for Continuous Internal State Modeling
- Operationalizing continuous state modeling in deep neural network backbones.
- Integrating internal regulatory loops (homeostatic coupling) into Transformer and State-Space Model (SSM) architectures.
- Proprioceptive feedback mechanisms and hierarchical action planning across internal regulatory and external environmental states.

#### 3. Homeostatic Coupling as an Intrinsic Foundation for AI Alignment and Safety
- Reframing AI safety away from post-hoc external reward tuning (e.g., RLHF) toward intrinsic internal regulatory dynamics.
- Establishing how prosocial behavior and safety can naturally emerge from intrinsic homeostatic constraints and empathic coupling.

#### 4. Community Consensus Roadmaps & New Benchmark Standards
- Defining new internal-alignment metrics: moving beyond static task accuracy toward measuring internal stability, self-monitoring fidelity, and regulatory resilience.
- A 5-year roadmap for constructing, benchmarking, and deploying dual-embodied foundation models.
